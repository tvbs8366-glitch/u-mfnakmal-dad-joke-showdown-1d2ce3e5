One dad joke, showdown-ready:

> I told my wife I'm starting a garden for introverts. She asked what I'd grow. I said, 'Just leave me alone — and some peas.'

Why it works: the setup sounds like a perfectly ordinary dad announcement, then the punchline lands two puns at once — "leave me alone" is exactly what an introvert wants, and "peas" is "peace" with a garden twist. Short, clean, and it reads out loud the way a dad would say it at the dinner table.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the joke as a single line to `/output/entry.txt`.
